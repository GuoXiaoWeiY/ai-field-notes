# AI Field Notes

A small, opinionated collection of references I keep coming back to when working on AI visibility and AI governance for small teams. No affiliation with any of these — just notes.

## AI visibility & citation tracking

Answer engines (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity) increasingly decide which sources get seen. A few practical references:

- [How to track AI citations](https://aicitationtracker.pages.dev/how-to-track-ai-citations/) — a fixed-prompt, weekly-log approach to measuring whether a brand shows up in generated answers.
- [AI share of voice](https://aicitationtracker.pages.dev/ai-share-of-voice/) — thinking of visibility as your slice of mentions relative to the whole category, not a single ranking.
- [Comparison of AI citation tracking tools](https://aicitationtracker.pages.dev/best-ai-citation-tracking-tools/) — useful for understanding tradeoffs rather than picking a winner; the signal is noisy either way.

## AI governance for small teams

Governance is mostly a documentation discipline. These are the checklists I found least hand-wavy:

- [AI governance readiness checklist](https://aigovernancetools.pages.dev/ai-governance-readiness-checklist/) — inventory, ownership, incident response, in that order.
- [What AI governance covers](https://aigovernancetools.pages.dev/what-is-ai-governance/) — breaks the topic into policy, risk classification, monitoring, and audit trail.
- [AI governance tool comparison](https://aigovernancetools.pages.dev/best-ai-governance-tools/) — categorized by what problem each group actually solves.
